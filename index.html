        From a live stream

        Record a stream that is live right now, or take a past stream from YouTube, Twitch, Kick or X, and turn its best moments into shorts.

        1Stream link
        2Find the best moments
        • Live now: records the next 5 minutes to 2 hours, then stops by itself. Stop recording keeps what's recorded so far.
        • Channel links work: e.g. youtube.com/@channel/live.
        • Past streams: set From / To to keep only the stretch you want; analysis is quicker.

        Guides

        Narrated videos made from a written guide. They're made in the background; this list follows them until they're ready.

        Create from a guide

        Paste a written guide with sections, each with a Visual and a Narración line, and get the finished narrated video with footage, music and subtitles.

        Number each section and give it a Visual: and a Narración: line. YouTube links in Visual are downloaded and shown under the voice. A parenthesis in the narration plays that clip with its own sound: add a time range like (Inserto 0:12-0:31) to pick the moment. Sections with a dash in the heading get a title on screen. The rest of the footage comes from Pexels.
        Your clips: on each section, Add link… takes a YouTube link (studio and Shorts links too) or a TikTok, Instagram, Facebook or X link, and Upload… takes files from this computer. With several clips in a section, (Video 2 0:05-0:20) picks the second one and (Video 2 completo) plays all of it.

          Import from YouTube

          Paste a YouTube video link. It downloads at the quality you pick, then the best moments are found and cut into clips.

          1Video link
          Only part of a long video?
          From / To keeps only that part of a long video or past stream (e.g. 1:05:00 to 1:35:00), so moments are found there and analysis is quicker. Times in the editor then count from “From”.
          2Find the best moments
          • Best quality takes up to 8K: a 9:16 crop of 8K is sharp enough for a 4K vertical export.
          • “Confirm you're not a bot”? Save your YouTube cookies as cookies.txt next to server.py and restart it.
          • Only import videos you have the rights to clip and repost.
