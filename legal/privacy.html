Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Clip Studio is a desktop tool that runs on your own computer. It has no servers of its own and does not collect, sell or share your personal data.

1. What the app stores

  • Your videos and clips stay on your computer. Transcription and editing run locally.
  • Access tokens for accounts you connect (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram) are saved only in a local file on your computer, so the app can publish when you ask it to.
  • Basic profile info from connected accounts (such as your TikTok username, display name and avatar, through the user.info.basic scope) is used only to show which account you're posting to.

2. What is sent to platforms

When you press Publish, the clip and its caption are uploaded directly from your computer to the platform you chose, using that platform's official API. Nothing else is sent, and nothing is sent to us.

3. Sharing

We do not share, sell or transfer any data to third parties. We do not use analytics, ads or tracking.

4. Deleting your data

Disconnect an account in the app to delete its saved tokens, or revoke access in the platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions). Deleting the app's folder removes everything it stored.
